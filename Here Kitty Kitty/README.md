# Here Kitty Kitty
## Scenario Details
This scenario moves the focus away from blunt brute-force entry and noisy perimeter breaches, plunging the incident response team into a carefully orchestrated campaign built around patience and psychological leverage. Rather than smashing through security controls, the threat actor establishes early contact through highly tailored communication channels, gradually building trust to bypass standard multi-factor authentication and identity boundaries.

Once inside the digital ecosystem, the adversary relies on lightweight scripting, living-off-the-land techniques, and abuse of cloud-hosted services to maintain access while quietly collecting high-value intelligence. Will the defensive team spot the subtle anomalies hidden within routine user sign-in logs and cloud application tokens, or will the quiet exfiltration of sensitive data go completely unnoticed until the damage is already done?

## Session Details
These sessions are open to all experience levels and are very beginner-friendly! This is a fun, educational group activity where you'll play a tabletop exercise modeled like a role-playing game to practice incident response. As a team, participants roll dice, leverage defensive security tools, and work together to investigate and contain a simulated cyber attack managed by a Game Master.
