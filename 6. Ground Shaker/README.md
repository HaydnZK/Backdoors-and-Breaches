# Ground Shaker (In Development)
## Operational Overview
This scenario pivots from the financially motivated exfiltration patterns of the Lazarus Group in From the Dead to the disruptive tradecraft of Sandworm. While our previous session forced us to hunt for unauthorized crypto-mining and lateral movement across cloud identities, Ground Shaker demands that we shift our focus straight to the enterprise network edge. By bypassing traditional Endpoint Detection and Response entirely, this simulation tests our visibility where threat actors operate directly out of core networking appliances and routing infrastructure.  

Will the attacker follow their destructive playbook with loud payloads, or will they opt for stealth through protocol abuse and firmware-level living-off-the-land techniques to silently intercept data? Expect to hunt for indicators of infrastructure compromise, anomalous traffic tunneling, and persistence mechanisms that survive reboots. It is time to see if our network visibility holds up when the adversary is living in the hardware.
